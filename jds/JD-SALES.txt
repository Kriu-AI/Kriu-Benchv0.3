The Opportunity
We are looking for an exceptional, quota-carrying Enterprise Account Executive to drive net-new enterprise software revenue across Fortune 500 and Global 2000 strategic accounts. In this role, you will quarterback complex, multi-threaded commercial sales campaigns from target account identification and C-suite outbound prospecting all the way to multi-stakeholder consensus, procurement navigation, and executed enterprise master agreements. Your deal sizes will typically range from $150,000 to over $1,000,000+ in annual contract value (ACV), involving consultative engagements with Chief Information Officers (CIOs), Chief Information Security Officers (CISOs), Chief Data Officers (CDOs), and VP-level engineering leadership.

Key Responsibilities & What You'll Do
- Execute a targeted, account-based outbound prospecting strategy into assigned named enterprise accounts, leveraging executive cold outreach, customized digital messaging, strategic referral networks, and personalized account research to generate qualified net-new opportunities.
- Systematically qualify every prospective enterprise engagement using the MEDDICC (or MEDDPICC) framework, rigorously verifying Metrics, Economic Buyer access, Decision Criteria, Decision Process, Identified Pain, Champion strength, and Competition at each sales stage.
- Manage an active, disciplined enterprise pipeline of 15 to 30 strategic opportunities, driving deals through discovery, solution validation, technical proof of concept (POC), executive business case presentation, and commercial procurement.
- Maintain impeccable CRM hygiene in Salesforce, ensuring that opportunity milestones, stakeholder contact mapping, close dates, next steps, and stage-by-stage deal amounts reflect real-time operational truth.
- Deliver highly predictable quarterly and annual sales forecasts to regional sales leadership and executive management, defending deal commitments, pipeline coverage ratios, and slip risks during weekly pipeline inspection reviews.
- Lead high-stakes commercial and legal contract negotiations, partnering closely with internal legal, finance, and info-sec teams to structure multi-year software licensing terms, master services agreements (MSAs), data processing addenda (DPAs), and pricing schedules.
- Orchestrate cross-functional account teams—including dedicated Sales Engineers (SEs), Enterprise SDRs, Professional Services consultants, and Customer Success Managers—to demonstrate measurable return on investment (ROI) to customer steering committees.
